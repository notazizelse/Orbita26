Can we still do every experiment?

✓Heartbeat through the shell (TCRT5000 → MCP6002 amplifier → PB0). Every part is on your list. The TCRT5000 comes off one HW-511.
✓Box air: temperature and humidity (GY-21 / SHT21) and pressure (BMP280).
✓Backup pulse sensor (MAX30100), after one check of its I²C pull-ups in step 10.
✗Heater at 20 °C + 40 °C hardware cutoff. You dropped it on purpose: insulation instead. You still have most of it (IRLZ44N, LM393, XT30, 2 × 150 Ω). Missing: the two 10 k NTC thermistors and the third 150 Ω. With two resistors it would be 75 Ω, 2.9 W at 14.8 V instead of 4.4 W.
✗ADS-B receiver idea. Not with these parts. It needs an SDR receiver for 1090 MHz, its antenna and a computer to decode. None of that is on this list.

With no heater, heart rate against box temperature becomes your main result. That makes where the SHT21 sits in the box air more important.

What your parts change in the build

  1. New step 1: measure every part and desolder the TCRT5000 from an HW-511. The second HW-511 stays whole as a spare sensor.
  2. C3 and C5 use your 104s. The old "still to buy" fallback is gone. The smaller 104s fit the tight spots better.
  3. All three of your 474s are used (C2, C11a, C11b), so you have none spare. If one cracks: C2 → five 104 in parallel (0.5 µF, low-pass 3.2 Hz). C11 → one 474 + four 104 (0.87 µF, corner 0.18 Hz). You have enough 104s for one of these fallbacks, not both.
  4. 1 MΩ is in your kit, so R15 stays as drawn.
  5. These don't go on the board: IRLZ44N, LM393P, 150 Ω 5 W, XT30, 1000 µF.
  6. Not on your list but still needed: perfboard, silicone wire, a 4-core cable for the TCRT5000, solder + wick, hot glue. A DIP-8 socket is optional.
