What this is, and isn't

This is a visual preview, not a fabrication file. Part sizes and footprints come from the schematic, but the board outline, positions and the traces painted on the surface are illustrative. The flight hardware for Orbita 2026 is built on perfboard from the same schematic. For the wiring, see hardware/SnailPulse_KiCad_Connections.xlsx; for the design, docs/Schematic_Review.md.

Off the board and not shown: the TCRT5000 sensor (U1), both NTC thermistors (TH1, TH2), the three 150 Ω 5 W heater resistors (R8–R10), and the SHT21, BMP280 and MAX30100 modules. They plug into J1, J3, J4 and J7–J9.
